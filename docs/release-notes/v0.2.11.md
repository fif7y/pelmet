# Pelmet 0.2.11

New:

- A Thanks tab in Settings. Pelmet is free and built by one person, and
  this is the one place it asks: sponsor on GitHub, star the repo (with
  the live count), follow @FIF7Y on X or @madebyfif7y on Instagram, or
  send the site to a friend through the share sheet. No account, no
  tracking, and the star count comes from GitHub's public endpoint with
  no key.

Fixed:

- A thin light line could flash along the bottom of the menu bar on the
  first hover reveal after using other apps. The reveal cover now spans
  the full bar window instead of stopping one point short.
