# Pelmet 0.2.26

Fixed:

- Turning on Media controls, Camera & mic, AirDrop or a Shortcut in
  Settings could crash Pelmet a few seconds after every launch, until a
  reinstall. The item was saved without a slot in the section order, and
  the first pass that read the bar tripped over it. Existing settings
  repair themselves on launch, nothing moves. Thanks to whbexc for the
  logs (#13).
