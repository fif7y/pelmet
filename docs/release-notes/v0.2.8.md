# Pelmet 0.2.8

New:

- Pick the menu bar icon. Settings › General offers six glyphs
  (chevron, arrow, eye, dots, grid, panel). Clicking one changes the
  icon right away. The chevron stays the default.
- The delay sliders snap to ticks. Hover delay runs 0.1 to 0.5 s in
  0.1 s steps, and the shortest setting really is 0.1 s now. Auto-rehide
  runs 0 to 5 s in half-second steps.

Changed:

- Settings › General is tidier: the "without the icon" hint sits in its
  own box, and Language has its own section.
