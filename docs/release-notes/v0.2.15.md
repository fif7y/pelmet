# Pelmet 0.2.15

Animation:

- The three styles now look and feel different in both directions. macOS
  animates every show and hide with a slide and a fade of its own, and
  Pelmet's styles now play over it: Instant shows the icons the moment
  the hover fires and drops them in one frame; Smooth slides them out from
  behind the chevron and tucks them back; Fade crossfades in and dissolves
  out in place. Without Screen Recording, icons show and hide the way
  macOS does it.
- Settings > Behavior opens with three cards, one per style, each playing a
  small mock of its animation. Only the active card plays; hover another to
  preview it.

Fixed:

- A quick hover on and off the menu bar sometimes left the icons showing
  for three seconds before they hid.
- After closing the Settings window, the first hovers over the menu bar did
  nothing until you clicked somewhere else.
- An app whose icon sat between the chevron and one of Pelmet's own items
  could flip to Hidden on its own after a collapse.
- The dot separator no longer nudges its neighbors while icons hide.
