# Pelmet 0.2.4

New:

- Pelmet now speaks ten languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian,
  Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian. It
  follows your system language, or pick one in Settings › General › Language.
  Translations are machine-drafted for now. Corrections are welcome in
  `scripts/gen-xcstrings.py`.

Fixes:

- Hovering out of the menu bar hides the icons again. Two always-on system
  and utility windows near the top of the screen were being mistaken for an
  open menu, which kept the bar revealed until you clicked somewhere else.
- The bar now stays revealed only while the Menu Bar tab of Settings is open.
  On the other tabs, and with Settings closed, auto-rehide behaves normally.
- ⌘-dragging an icon in the menu bar reliably updates its order in the layout
  editor, including on multi-display setups.
- Pelmet's own items (media controls, separators) land in their assigned slot
  after a relaunch instead of wherever macOS parked them.
- The editor names Pelmet's own tiles Media, Camera and AirDrop instead of
  "Pelmet".
- The Settings window opens taller, and long sidebar labels no longer wrap.
