# Pelmet 0.2.36

Fixed:

- A window parked right under the menu bar left its dark edge in the bar
  on every hover reveal after you moved the window away (#33, thanks @oantonov). The menu
  bar is glass, and the picture Pelmet floats while icons come in carried
  the old backdrop. Pelmet now retakes that picture as soon as a window
  moves near the bar.
- Relaunching an app in the Always hidden section flashed every hidden
  icon into the bar for a moment (#31, thanks @oantonov). macOS only lets a fresh icon in
  while nothing is hidden, so Pelmet has to let go for an instant; that
  instant is now covered like the clock click already was.
- An icon macOS itself takes down while Pelmet hides yours, because its
  app runs from outside the Applications folder, vanished from Settings
  too (#30). It keeps its tile, marked can't hide, so you can still see
  it is there and launch it.
- Clicking the purple privacy indicator to the right of the clock revealed
  the hidden icons.
- Separators: the invisible spacer draws its gap as a dashed slot in the
  editor and in the chooser, and its popover offers a width control in
  place of opacity.
- A Pelmet items helper killed at login was never relaunched, leaving its
  separator without a host until Pelmet restarted. Helper deaths are now
  caught at the kernel and the helper comes back within a fraction of a
  second.
- Quitting Pelmet with the Focus item on left a log reader running.
