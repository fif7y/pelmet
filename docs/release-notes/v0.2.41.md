# Pelmet 0.2.41

New:

- An opt-in beta channel. Settings › About › "Get beta releases" turns on
  smaller, more frequent updates. Leave it off and you get one stable
  release every week or two that rolls the betas up, one set of notes at a
  time (#48, thanks @vorssaint). Turning it off later never downgrades: the
  next stable simply takes over.

Fixed:

- A click Pelmet refused (on a status item's popover edge, right of the
  chevron, on the pinned right zone) no longer counts toward a double-click.
  Before, a refused click followed by a real empty-bar click inside the
  double-click interval revealed Always Hidden (#47, thanks
  @ChoSeongmin1128).
