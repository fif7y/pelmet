# Pelmet 0.2.31

Fixed:

- The strip where your icons hide could flash a slightly different shade
  than the rest of the bar while they tuck away or come back, with a light
  line along the bar's bottom edge on the first hover after switching
  apps. The picture Pelmet floats over the bar during the move came from
  the system's screenshot call, which leaves out the shadow and edge the
  front window casts up into the bar's bottom rows. It now comes from a
  single frame of the screen stream and matches the live bar to the pixel
  (#25, and the white line some of you saw on hover reveals).
- The Kerberos ticket menu can be hidden. It stays where macOS puts it,
  like Siri and Time Machine (#24, thanks @riarheos).
