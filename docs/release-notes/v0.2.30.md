# Pelmet 0.2.30

New:

- A Timer item. macOS's own timer is one of the Control Center items the
  system pulls out of the bar whenever any icon is hidden, and nothing
  outside Apple's apps can read it, so Pelmet runs its own: click for a
  duration (1 minute to an hour, or Custom…), it counts down in the bar,
  rings and posts a banner at zero, and it stays in the bar while your
  icons are hidden. Pause, add a minute, cancel from its menu. A relaunch
  keeps it counting. It can't mirror a timer started in Clock or with
  Siri (#22).
- A Fast user switching item, since macOS hides that menu the same way:
  other users, Login Window, Lock Screen, Users & Groups.
- Both are in Settings › Menu Bar › Pelmet items, and the FAQ names every
  item macOS hides on its own.

Fixed:

- After an update (or any quick quit-and-relaunch), Pelmet could come back
  without its hidden separators: the previous instance's helper processes
  were still winding down and the new Pelmet adopted them instead of
  starting fresh. Helpers now exit the instant Pelmet does, and a leftover
  one is replaced.
- A hidden-section separator's position after a reveal was read as your
  arrangement and could reorder the section; it holds its slot now, like
  every other Pelmet item.
- Fewer accessibility walks and window-list reads per hover reveal.
