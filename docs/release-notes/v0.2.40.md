# Pelmet 0.2.40

Fixed:

- A full menu bar is left alone. When your icons don't fit and macOS tucks
  some behind its own «, Pelmet no longer drags them back, clicks that «
  with your pointer, or keeps asking the system to re-show them. The
  Menu Bar tab now says how many icons sit behind the « (#42, thanks
  @OiCkilL).
- macOS's own menu bar agents are never managed. The stop button of a
  screen recording, and any other icon that comes from a system agent
  rather than an app, stays where the system put it instead of being
  routed to Hidden (#42, thanks @OiCkilL).
- The camera & mic indicator stays where it lands when the mic comes on.
  It no longer drags itself to its exact slot on your next reveal, which
  read as a laggy reveal and an icon moving on its own (#39, thanks
  @GianAndris).
- While a Pelmet item stands in for one of macOS's own extras (camera &
  mic, media, AirDrop, Focus, Timer, users), the system's version stays
  hidden even with System extras set to "Show while revealed". Before,
  the two swapped places on every hover (#39, thanks @GianAndris; #41,
  thanks @archellino).
- Pelmet's own context menu no longer ends up in the bar pictures. A
  picture taken while the menu was closing could replay the menu's top
  edge on the next hover (#40, thanks @richyueh).
- The bar picture is retaken when Pelmet's icon has moved since it was
  taken, so a placement can't leave a second chevron on screen for a
  beat.
