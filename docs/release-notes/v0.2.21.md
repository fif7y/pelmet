# Pelmet 0.2.21

Right-clicking an empty spot in the menu bar opens Pelmet's menu. That's
useful when the Pelmet icon is hidden, but it also meant Pelmet was taking a
click that sometimes belonged to something else. If another app draws its own
surface up there, a notch app for instance, they're both after the same
right-click.

Two changes for that.

Added:

- A switch for the right-click menu, in Settings > General. Turn it off and
  an empty-space right-click is left alone.
- Right-clicking a Pelmet separator still opens the menu, and so does the
  shortcut. You don't lose your way in.
- The switch stays on while the Pelmet icon is hidden. With no icon in the
  bar that right-click is your way back into Settings, so Pelmet won't let
  you close both doors at once.

Fixed:

- A right-click on another app's window in the menu bar now goes to that app.
  Pelmet was reading any window up there as empty bar. Thanks to
  xX-Mordran-Xx for reporting it.
- When Pelmet loses its Accessibility permission the icon turns orange to
  tell you. On macOS 27 that colour never reached the screen and the icon
  went black instead. On a dark menu bar that's close to invisible, so the
  warning's been silent since the day it was added.
- The Pelmet icon could go missing for good after you restore your settings
  from a backup or another Mac. macOS keeps each icon's visibility next to
  Pelmet's own preferences, so a backup taken while the bar was collapsed
  brought the icon back hidden. There's no telling from Settings either, the
  switch still reads on.
