# Pelmet 0.3.0-beta.6

Sixth beta of 0.3.0, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases", 0.2.41 or later). Everything from beta.5, plus one fix.

Sorry for the back-to-back betas. I introduced a reveal bug in the last
builds and it needed fixing today rather than tomorrow.

Fixed:

- On reveal, the last icon of the hidden section could show cut in half,
  then the whole strip jumped into place once the icons were out. The
  picture Pelmet paints during a reveal landed 16pt off. It measured how
  far the bar had moved from Pelmet's own status item, and that item
  doesn't read at the same spot while the bar is collapsed. It now
  measures from the icon next to it, which stays put.
