# Pelmet 0.2.10

Fixed:

- Hovering another app's menu bar overlay no longer reveals the hidden
  section. Pelmet treated any pointer resting in the menu bar band as a
  bar hover, so opening a notch app like Sconce made the icons to its
  right fade in and re-render about a second later. The hover reveal now
  checks which window is actually under the pointer and stays put when
  it belongs to another app. Clicks and drags are unchanged.
