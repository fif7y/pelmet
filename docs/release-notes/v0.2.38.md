# Pelmet 0.2.38

Changed:

- The hidden icons are summoned from one stretch of the bar now: from the
  middle of the screen to Pelmet's icon, where they land. Hovering or
  clicking anywhere right of the icon — your visible icons, Control
  Center, the clock, the recording dot — no longer opens them (#37, thanks
  @brodly). The icon itself still toggles on click, and the hotkey is
  unchanged.
- Every Apple menu bar icon that belongs to an app of its own — Weather,
  Passwords, the Kerberos ticket, and whatever Apple ships next — is
  managed like any third-party icon: hideable and movable, no per-app
  patch (#36, thanks @mr-steveryan for the push to generalise it). Only
  the icons macOS hosts itself (Wi-Fi, Sound, battery, the clock, Control
  Center…) keep their own rules. Passwords turns out to be movable after
  all; its lock badge is gone.

Fixed:

- Clicking the clock while the Settings window was open flashed every
  icon. The still Pelmet floats over the bar for that click now covers the
  always-hidden icons too.
- A click on the recording dot right of the clock opens Notification
  Center like a click on the clock (#37, thanks @brodly). The pointer stays
  where you clicked.

Added:

- Behavior › System extras: "Clicking the clock opens Notification
  Center", on by default. macOS refuses that click while icons are
  hidden, so Pelmet lets go for an instant and clicks for you; without
  Screen Recording that instant shows. Off, the clock is left alone and
  the two-finger swipe from the trackpad's right edge still opens it.
