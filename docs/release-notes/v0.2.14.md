# Pelmet 0.2.14

Fixed:

- Hiding icons no longer drags the wallpaper along with them. macOS fades
  hidden icons out on its own, and Pelmet's extra hide animation on top of it
  had started to show: a smear of background in the Smooth style, a small
  jump of the chevron in Fade. Pelmet now leaves the exit to macOS. The
  Smooth, Fade and Instant styles still shape how icons come back.
- Pelmet's separators and its own extras no longer nudge their neighbors
  while everything fades out.

Added:

- Screen Recording shows up next to Accessibility in Settings > General >
  Permissions, and as an optional second row in onboarding, with a plain
  explanation of what it is for (the Fade and Instant reveal styles). It is
  never required.
