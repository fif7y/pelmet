# Pelmet 0.3.0-beta.3

Third beta of 0.3.0, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases", 0.2.41 or later). Everything from beta.2, plus:

Fixed:

- Clicking the clock while icons are hidden showed a lighter rectangle
  over the hidden section while Notification Center slid open, and a
  darker one while it closed. Closing no longer blinks at all, the click
  just goes through. Opening keeps its picture of the bar only until the
  panel has slid in, then shows the bar as it looks under the panel; the
  picture also stops short of the visible icons instead of running to
  the clock (#51, thanks @richyueh).

- Changing the wallpaper or switching light and dark left the old bar in
  the picture for up to fifteen minutes, on every reveal and on every
  clock click. Both now drop the picture at once.

- On a Mac with a Notification Center widget on the desktop, Pelmet read
  the panel as always open, so a clock press that missed was never
  retried.
