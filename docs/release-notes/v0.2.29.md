# Pelmet 0.2.29

New:

- Keyboard shortcuts. ⌥⌘, shows or hides the hidden icons, ⇧⌥⌘, opens
  Settings. Both can be changed in Settings › General; ⌫ or the × puts
  the default back (#18).
- Media controls can animate: three bars that wave while audio plays and
  settle when it pauses. Off by default, Static or Animated per item in
  Settings › Pelmet items. Animated costs about 3% CPU while music plays.
- Hidden and always-hidden separators are hosted by two small helper
  processes inside Pelmet.app, so they hide with everything else instead
  of fading on their own clock. Nothing to set up; a section move in the
  editor moves the separator between hosts.
- Pelmet's own icon and separators can be dragged off the bar like any
  other item.
- Siri and Time Machine hide as one tile and show a "Pinned by macOS"
  card in the editor, since macOS keeps them where they are (#19).
- The hover reveal listens on the right half of the bar only, so moving
  across the left half no longer pops the hidden icons (#21).

Fixed:

- Settings stayed alive after its window closed and kept animating off
  screen at up to 27% CPU (#20).
- A menu bar icon that refuses to be moved (some Electron apps swallow
  the drag) is left where its app put it after three tries, with a note
  in the editor, instead of being dragged at every reveal (#15).
- Wide status items with a title are identified by their full title, no
  500pt cap (#17, thanks @riarheos).
- A separator moved between sections in the editor stayed with its old
  host until the next settings change, which could let other hidden
  separators through.
- Clicking the clock always opens Notification Center now; the toggle is
  gone.
- A Pelmet item placed after a pinned Apple icon no longer bounces
  between two slots at every reveal.
