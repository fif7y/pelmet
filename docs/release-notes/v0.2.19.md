# Pelmet 0.2.19

Placement got a rethink. Icons should stay where you put them, and if one
ever ends up on the wrong side of the chevron, Pelmet now notices and puts
it back.

Fixed:

- After a hover, a hidden icon could show up on the visible side of the
  chevron and stay there. The cause was one of Pelmet's own items (the
  camera indicator, the media control) being placed while the hidden
  icons were collapsed. Pelmet no longer moves anything in the hidden zone
  while it's collapsed. Those moves wait for the next reveal.
- New: an order check runs at every reveal. Any icon sitting on the wrong
  side of the chevron for its section is dragged back, right there, with a
  second measurement first so a bar still settling is never misjudged.
- Editor drops could land one slot off, or not move the icon at all. It
  happened when the app had a copy of its icon on another display, or
  reported its icon under two names. Pelmet now measures the copy on the
  main display.
- Relaunching a menu bar app that runs without a Dock icon didn't put its
  icon back in its slot. It does now.
- Placement aims at the middle of the two neighbors from the start. That's
  where the retry was landing every time. The result is read once the bar
  has stopped moving, not at a fixed delay. Fewer drags, fewer bounces.
- A hidden icon at the end of its section could pass the placement check
  while sitting right of the chevron. The chevron is now the bound.
